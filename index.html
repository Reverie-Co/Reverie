What is Reverie Lab

Reverie Lab is an independent research umbrella for work at the intersection of artificial intelligence, biological systems, and compute infrastructure.

The goal is not to build the next app. It's to work on the layer beneath — the infrastructure, tools, and systems that determine what AI can become. Less energy. More capability. Closer to how nature actually computes.

NeuroBridge

Compute infrastructure for neuromorphic AI

AI today runs on hardware that wastes most of its energy. Brain-inspired chips that are 100× more efficient exist — but no software layer connects them to the frameworks developers already use. NeuroBridge is that missing layer.

The Problem
AI compute is fundamentally broken
GPU-based AI inference is 100× more energy-intensive than biological neural computation. Datacenter energy costs are scaling out of control globally — and AI demand keeps growing.
The Gap
The hardware exists. The software doesn't.
Chips like Intel Loihi 2 and IBM NorthPole are 25–100× more efficient than GPU equivalents. But there is no software layer connecting them to mainstream AI. The hardware is ready. The tooling isn't.
What others tried
Why it hasn't worked — until now
Intel's Lava framework requires developers to learn entirely new paradigms. IBM's TrueNorth can't run standard models. Academic SNN research stays in papers. Nobody built the bridge between worlds.
The Solution
The CUDA moment for neuromorphic
NeuroBridge converts PyTorch models to event-driven spiking networks. Benchmark energy vs GPU. Deploy on brain-inspired hardware. No rewrites. No new paradigms. Just plug in and measure.

🧬 Research Project · Biology

CellClock

A home laboratory experiment exploring whether synthetic genetic circuits can influence how long cells live — replicating and extending a 2023 Science paper showing 82% replicative lifespan extension.

The Question
Can we reprogram how long a cell lives by engineering the genetic circuits that control its internal clock?
The Method
GFP-tagged yeast cells with a synthetic genetic oscillator, tracked via automated microscopy and AI-driven image analysis over time.
The Vision
A closed-loop system where AI observes, analyzes, and eventually guides biological intervention autonomously — bringing compute to biology.
The Connection
The same principles behind NeuroBridge — efficient, adaptive computation — applied to understanding how biological systems age and adapt.
